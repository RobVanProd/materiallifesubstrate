handle, composition, exact derived element inventory/mass/heat capacity,
structural/stored/thermal energy and sole 102-byte B96 phase payload. Those
packets allocate no legacy position, momentum or remainder lanes. An explicit
mechanics-ID to material-handle/generation binding determines relation endpoints.
Fixture material IDs have the opposite storage order to mechanics IDs; reversing

records. Heat support uses exact decoded B96 relative geometry; no legacy
coordinate is read. The synthetic monatomic fixture has the preregistered raw

ledger. A legacy combined exact-conservation report is deliberately unavailable;
no bounded residual is converted into thermal/stored energy or called exact.
