# Research hypotheses and falsifiers

The program is valuable when it can fail. These hypotheses are staged; evidence
for an earlier one does not establish a later one.

## H1 — Substrate composability

Generic low-level matter laws support diverse mechanical, transport, thermal,
chemical, and field affordances without named high-level functions.

**Falsified or blocked when:** a required gauntlet construction needs a semantic
special case, privileged material class, target-specific operator, or observer
feedback; materially similar structures cannot combine their effects; or useful
function is a grid/timestep artifact.

## H2 — Seeded-life evolvability

At MLS-1, a declared seeded minimal reproducer can evolve viable changes to
metabolism, morphology, sensing, control, and ecological interaction while all
construction and selection remain physical.

**Falsified or blocked when:** persistence depends on hidden cloning, spawn or
despawn, high-level reward/fitness, semantic sensing, free matter/energy, fixed
body-part operations, or an interpreter that directly implements the claimed
innovation.

## H3 — Causal niche construction

Heritable environmental modification changes evolutionary trajectories beyond
the cost and behavior of performing the modifying action.

**Falsified or unsupported when:** replicated real-write worlds do not differ on
preregistered measures from equal-action, equal-energy shams in which only the
environmental write is suppressed, or when a difference disappears under replay
and exploit quarantine.

## H4 — Scaling expands the frontier

Increasing available world volume, matter, time, and compute expands the frontier
of durable innovations without adding named affordances or changing the
no-cheating ABI.

**Falsified or unsupported when:** innovation saturates across justified scale
increases; gains require new semantic components, target APIs, camera-dependent
LOD, or destructive coarse-graining; or apparent gains vanish under matched
compute/null comparisons.

## H5 — Local learning changes trajectories

Physically local plastic signaling lets organisms acquire within-lifetime state
that causally changes survival, construction, or reproduction and subsequently
changes evolutionary trajectories.

**Falsified or unsupported when:** matched no-plasticity controls produce the same
preregistered trajectories, learned effects require global labels/reward or
nonlocal writes, or the apparent memory is inherited initialization rather than
within-lifetime physical change.

## H6 — Cumulative external affordances

Useful learned or copied external structures/behaviors persist, transmit between
organisms, undergo modification, and combine into higher-order durable
affordances.

**Falsified or unsupported when:** external effects are ephemeral or genetically
recreated rather than transmitted; copying cannot be separated from independent
rediscovery; modifications do not accumulate; combinations require a crafting
API; or complexity measures rise without demonstrated causal function.

## Inference discipline

- Pre-register operational measures, null models, replication counts, and stop
  rules before looking at outcome labels.
- Report negative and saturated results.
- Use observer language such as “organism,” “tool,” and “culture” only with an
  explicit operational definition.
- An H6 result is not automatically open-ended evolution; an H2 result is not
  abiogenesis; an H1 result is not life.
